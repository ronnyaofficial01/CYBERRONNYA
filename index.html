About Me

I am Rohan Salunke, a passionate cybersecurity expert, YouTuber, and student from Dhanora, a small village near Takli Kolte in the Phulambri block of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra, India. Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s in Computer Science at Fergusson College, Pune—a renowned institution with a sprawling 65-acre campus, historic architecture, and vibrant academic environment—I am dedicated to advancing cybersecurity in India through innovation and education.

My Journey

My journey into technology began in 5th grade (2015–2017) when I explored games and experimented with my parents’ devices. At Z.P.P. School in Dhanora, I cherished some of my most formative years. A pivotal moment came when I received a Raspberry Pi from the Pratham Digital Foundation, introducing me to Linux. Navigating its command-line interface was daunting, but my curiosity drove me to learn. Using YouTube tutorials, I installed Wine to run games, overcoming technical challenges and building foundational skills.

YouTube’s algorithm soon introduced me to cybersecurity and ethical hacking content, sparking a deep interest. After scoring 92% in my 10th-grade SSC exams, I enrolled in Government Polytechnic, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. My passion for cybersecurity led me to prioritize self-study over college. I invested in a laptop—a significant milestone for my family—and immersed myself in advanced ethical hacking techniques. This focus caused backlogs in my second semester, prompting me to pursue my 11th and 12th grades while completing two years of intensive cybersecurity training.

During this period, I worked part-time at CyberIndians, editing videos for their YouTube channel (CyberIndians YouTube) and contributing to their mission (CyberIndians Website). I also completed a 6-month internship as a technical supporter, gaining practical experience. Now, at Fergusson College, I balance academics with cybersecurity projects, driven by a vision to transform India’s digital security landscape.

My Mission

India faces a critical cybersecurity crisis, with scams costing an estimated ₹20,000 crore annually, while the national cybersecurity budget is a mere ₹1,900 crore, with only ₹300–700 crore allocated to core initiatives. Maharashtra’s cyber cell often lacks the expertise to address modern threats, such as tracing lost devices or leveraging Linux-based tools. My mission is to revolutionize cybersecurity in India by developing innovative solutions, raising awareness, and bridging the gap between technology and law enforcement. Through my YouTube channel, CYBERRONNYA, I aim to educate and empower individuals to navigate the digital world securely.
